# Release and evidence

## Before merge/release

Verify linked Linear issue, acceptance criteria, tests/CI, migration safety, secret exposure, accessibility impact, rollout and rollback path.

## Human approval

TL and QA approve merge/release. An agent reports evidence and risks; it never deploys or merges automatically.

## Final documentation

Distinguish planned, implemented and verified outcomes. Cite Linear, PR, test, ADR or release evidence for each material claim.
