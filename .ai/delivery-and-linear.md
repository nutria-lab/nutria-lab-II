# Delivery and Linear

## Source of truth

Linear tracks project status, issues, cycle, estimate, owner, blockers and linked PR/test evidence. Use a time tracker linked to Linear issues for actual hours.

## Weekly report

Report commitment vs completion, estimate vs actual hours, time in status, blockers, risk owner, quality status and recommended next-cycle capacity.

## Rules

- Estimates calibrate complexity/capacity; hours are not a productivity score.
- Do not infer hours from commits, messages or agent activity.
- Agents read first and propose changes; PM/PO approval is required for scope, priority, dates, status or assignment mutations.
